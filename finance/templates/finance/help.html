Help

What each screen does, in one place. For anything about how the app is wired up behind the scenes — connecting accounts, scheduling, backups — see the runbook in the repo.

Home

Your own mix of widgets — net worth, balances, budgets, recent activity, what's waiting for review. Choose which ones show and in what order from Preferences; David and Maddie can each set up a different homepage.

Activity

Every transaction, filterable by account, category, date, or search. A category badged "unsure" is the classifier's best guess at a low confidence — confirming it teaches the app for next time, so the same merchant is never asked about twice. For a standing rule (matching more than one merchant, or beyond an exact match), add it directly from Settings → Category rules.

Charts

Every chart shares one date range and resolution (weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually — whichever the selected range actually covers), except Balances over time and budget attainment, which follow their own daily or budget-anchored timelines. Net income counts every deposit categorised as income, whether or not a payslip was ever imported for it; gross pay and tax only show up as optional extra detail where a payslip has been imported. Recurring expenses tracks merchants with spend in at least three periods in the window — a subscription or a utility creeping up shows up as its own line. Largest transactions surfaces the biggest one-off outflows. Click a bar in any chart to see exactly which transactions produced it.

Use "Hide chart" on any section to tuck it away, and reopen it from the "Hidden charts" list at the bottom of the page — or reorder and choose which sections show at all from Preferences.

Budgets

A target over a repeating window, across one or more categories and optionally specific accounts. The bar shows pace against an even spend rate, not just percent-used — the number that actually answers "can we afford this today." "View transactions" on any budget opens exactly what counted toward it.

Settings

Connect an institution, manage existing connections, correct an account's type, and see recent sync history. Category rules created from the review queue live here too.

Imports

For anything no bank sync reaches — the mortgage, the life policy, payslips. Upload a CSV, confirm the detected columns, review the preview, then commit. A confirmed mapping is remembered, so the next statement from the same source is one click.

Alerts & reports

Alerts are yours alone — a balance crossing a line, a budget running hot, or a source that's gone quiet (useful for both a broken connector and a manual account overdue for a fresh statement). Reports are a weekly or monthly email summary; "send me one now" previews it before you commit to a schedule.

QFRs

A Quarterly Finance Report: a closed quarter's numbers plus a short written summary, with comparisons to the previous quarter and the same quarter last year. Generated after a quarter ends, not live — see the runbook for how to produce one.

Preferences

Personal, not shared: homepage widgets, which accounts and budgets show by default, how many recent transactions to list. Nothing here changes what the other person sees.
