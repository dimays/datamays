Connect an institution

  1. 1. Go to bridge.simplefin.org and authorize the institution.
  2. 2. Copy the setup token it gives you.
  3. 3. Paste it below. We'll exchange it, store it encrypted, and pull the last 90 days as a test.

The token becomes a read-only access URL. It can list balances and transactions and nothing else — it can never move money — and you can revoke it from SimpleFIN at any time without touching your bank password.
