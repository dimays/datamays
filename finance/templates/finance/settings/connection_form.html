Connect an institution

  1. 1. Go to bridge.simplefin.org and authorize one or more institutions — SimpleFIN lets you link several to a single setup token, and this form doesn't need you to say which up front.
  2. 2. Copy the setup token it gives you.
  3. 3. Paste it below. We'll exchange it, store it encrypted, and pull the last 90 days as a test — each account gets matched to its own institution automatically.

The token becomes a read-only access URL. It can list balances and transactions and nothing else — it can never move money — and you can revoke it from SimpleFIN at any time without touching your bank password.
