Help

How each part of Mays Household works. Finance has its own help page.

Today

Where you land after signing in. Anything overdue comes first, in red, then what's due today, then the week's one-offs and less-frequent chores — the filter change, the registration renewal — so they aren't a surprise. Daily and weekly routines are left out of "coming up" on purpose. Below them are your budgets, then projects in progress. The red number on the Chores tab is your overdue count, shown on every page.

Chores

Your checklist, the shared household chores either of you can do, and — with Show Maddie's chores / Show David's chores switched on — the other person's. Tap the circle to mark something done; tap Undo if you didn't mean to.

You can mark the other person's chore done too — it asks first, and records that you did it. Only a chore's owner can edit, skip, or delete it, unless they've ticked Let the other person manage this chore. Shared chores — and every upkeep item and project task — either of you can manage.

How chores repeat

A chore can happen once (with or without a due date), or repeat daily, weekly on the days you pick, monthly (on a date, or "the second Saturday", or "the last Friday"), or yearly. The form shows what your choices mean as you make them.

On a fixed schedule follows the calendar: if this week's bins are still open when next week's are due, this week's is marked missed and next week's takes its place — a week away leaves one item, not seven, marked with how many were missed. Counting from when it was last done waits for you: the next furnace filter is due 90 days after the last one was actually changed, however late that was.

A due date is when a chore shows up; a deadline is when it's late. A season ("only April to October") skips the months outside it. To stop a chore for a while without losing its history, edit it and untick Active.

Upkeep

Shared home maintenance. Each item has where it is, how to do it, the supplies it needs, and what it usually costs, and it lands on a checklist when it's due. Mark it done from its page to record what it cost and a note; later, Link the purchase ties the job to the real transaction from Finance. The top of the page estimates what upkeep will cost over the next 90 days and 12 months. The starter list has common jobs to add in one tap.

Projects

Longer-term house projects. Add milestones with dates to build the timeline, links to documents that live elsewhere (Drive, quotes), and notes as you decide things. Tasks are chores: give one to either of you and it lands on their checklist and their Today.

Under Budget, add lines for what you plan to spend, then Link spending to pick the real purchases from Finance. It suggests home-related spending around the project's dates; search by merchant for anything else. A refund linked to the same project counts back against the spending.

Morning digest

Switch it on in Household preferences for a short email after 7am: overdue and today's chores, the week ahead, and project milestones coming up. Nothing is sent on a morning with nothing to say.
